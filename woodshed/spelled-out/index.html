Spelled Out

Count the letters first. Then count the frets.

Cheat sheet

Step 1, letters. The number in an interval's name is how many letters to count, with the starting letter as 1. A 3rd above C: C(1) D(2) E(3). Sharps and flats don't change the count, so a 3rd above C♯ is still some kind of E.

Step 2, frets. The quality (major, minor, perfect) says how many frets apart the notes are. Check the plain letter, then add a sharp or flat to land on the right fret.

IntervalLettersFretsFrom C

Chords. Most chords stack every other letter (C E G B, D F A C). Get those letters down first, then fix each one with Step 2.

Keyboard: A–G for letters, # sharp, - flat, = natural, Backspace to clear, Enter to check or move on.
